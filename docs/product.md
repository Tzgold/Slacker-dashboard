# Product direction

Slacker is a lightweight Chrome extension that brings Telegram-style send and seen indicators to Slack Web.

- One check means a message was sent and tracked.
- Two teal checks mean another Slacker user opened the conversation.
- Both people need Slacker installed for Seen status to work.

The marketing page keeps the explanation honest: Slacker is independent from Slack, does not require a separate account, and does not upload full message content.
